SCHOOL OF SAINT ANTHONY

WHAT WE STAND FOR.

SAPIENTIA ET VIRTUS

The school adheres to the philosophy of Saint Anthony Mary Claret – “to train the heart, mind, and body to reap great achievements through great values in life.”

Mission Statement

Guided by the motto "Sapientia et Virtus" which means "Wisdom coupled with Virtues" the institution fulfills its educational activities in cooperation with other sectors by:

  • providing quality instruction and professional leadership in the integral development of individuals as social beings;
  • creating an academic community where students' love for learning is always coupled with the practice of virtues; and
  • producing graduates with a deep sense of social responsibility and involvement in responding to the needs of society.

Vision Statement

Aligning itself with the mission of the Church through the inspiration of St. Anthony Mary Claret's genuine love for God, It envisions Anthonians with wisdom and virtues.
