Air Fryer Lasagna: A Pinoy Twist

Lasagna is an is a classic Italian pasta dish made of stacked layers of wide, flat pasta sheets, alternating with fillings such as meat sauce (ragù or bolognese), a cheese mixture (often ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan), vegetables, and sometimes a béchamel sauce.

What are you? An idiot sandwich.

- Chef Gordon Ramsay, THE chef

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna Pasta 750g
  • Pinoy Style Spaghetti Sauce 900g
  • Butter 2 tbsp
  • Ground Pork 500g
  • Garlic 1/2 clove
  • Onion 1 pc
  • Fresh Milk
  • Flour

Preparation:

  1. Boil the pasta in a pot for about 8 minutes, or until al dente.
  2. In a pan, saute onion and garlic using butter until light brown.
  3. Add ground pork. Follow up with spaghetti sauce when the ground pork is cooked.
  4. Add salt, pepper, sugar, and pasta water, to taste.
  5. In a separate pan, melt 2 tbsp of butter. Slowly introduce flour and keep adding until desired consistency.
  6. Add milk in the flour and butter mixture, until desired consistency. Add salt, pepper, and sugar, to taste.
  7. In an air fryer-safe container, assemble the lasagna layers. Pasta - sauce - bechamel then repeat for each layer.
  8. Add cheese on top of the assembled lasagna.
  9. Cook in the air fryer for 15 minutes @ 180C.
  10. Serve and enjoy.

Cooking Tips:

  • Do not panic when making bechamel (not that I did). It is normal for the mixture to go dry. Keep adding milk until desired consistency.
  • Best use glass container for air frying. An alternative are the disposable molded foils.
