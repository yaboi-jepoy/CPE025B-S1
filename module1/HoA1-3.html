This is: IV of SPADES

IV of Spades (pronounced as Four of Spades) is a Filipino rock band, consisting of Zild Benitez, Blaster Silonga, and Badjao de Castro, with Unique Salonga promoting with the band. Known for their style and aesthetic, as well as their punk rock sound, the band gained mainstream popularity and exposure with the release of their 4th single "Mundo".

IV of Spades is heavily inspired by 1970s fashion, acts and sounds. Members of the band have cited various musical groups and artists as songwriting influences, including ABBA, Kings of Leon, Tame Impala, My Chemical Romance, Radiohead and Jet. The band's genre is a mix of 1970s influenced disco, funk, alternative rock and Manila Sound. The band also covered some songs of The Beatles like "Something" and "Hey Jude".
