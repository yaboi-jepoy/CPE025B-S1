Top Three Beginner Guitars

Stratocaster

The round-sounding, hollowed mids that captured 80's Rock scene.

Telecaster

A sharp, tangy, and Blues-weapon of a guitar.

Les Paul

Heavy, punchy, and with high presence. The ultimate Rock 'n Roll Guitar.

Personally, I like the sound and character of a Stratocaster, that's why it's my first guitar. Although I also have a Telecaster, but I find it a little bit harder to shape into my desired sound.
